# Rogue Assistant — v0.2.0-beta.1

A full redesign: two weapon buttons that apply poisons, stones and oils from your bags, in or out of combat.

> ⚠️ **Beta:** everything is covered by automated tests, but **nothing has run in the game client yet**. The item ids are Classic Era ids that are not confirmed for Forever, so something in your bags may not be offered on the first try. Please include the output of `/ras debug bags` and `/ras debug weapon` when you report a problem.

## ✨ What's new

- **Two weapon buttons:** main hand (MH) and off hand (OH), each showing what the weapon carries
- **Item list:** click a weapon to see the poisons, sharpening stones, weightstones and weapon oils in your bags that fit it, strongest first, with how many you have. Sharpening stones are offered only for edged weapons and weightstones only for blunt ones
- **One click to apply:** click an item to apply it to that weapon. Opening the list and applying work in combat too; the list is rebuilt when the fight ends, and a spent item is greyed out meanwhile
- **Timer and charges:** the time left under each button and the charges above it
- **Warning:** a red glow when a weapon has nothing applied or less than 5 minutes left. Change the threshold with `/ras warn <minutes>`
- **Movable bar:** `/ras unlock`, drag the handle, `/ras lock`. `/ras reset` puts it back
- English and Spanish texts

## 💥 Removed

- The cooldown and aura tracker, the poison reminders and the options panel are gone. Settings of earlier versions are discarded once
