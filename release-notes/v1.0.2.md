# Rogue Assistant — v1.0.2

A fix for Crippling Poison.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Crippling Poison not detected:** Crippling Poison (and Crippling Poison II) now shows under the weapon with its timer once applied. It has no charges, so the addon took it for a spent poison and hid it, or showed it as a stone
