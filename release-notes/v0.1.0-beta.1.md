# Rogue Assistant — First Beta (v0.1.0-beta.1)

A cooldown and aura tracker and a one-button poison menu for rogues in **World of Warcraft: Forever**.

> ⚠️ **Beta:** everything is covered by automated tests, but **nothing has run in the game client yet**. Several spell and item ids are Classic Era ids that are not confirmed for Forever, so an icon may be missing or a poison may not be recognised on the first try. Please run `/ras debug probe` (and `/ras debug bags`, `/ras debug spells`, `/ras debug auras`) and include the output when you report a problem.

## ✨ Features

### Poison button
- One button holds every poison in your bags. Click it to open the list (top rank of each type first, with the count you carry)
- **Left click** a poison to apply it to your main hand, **right click** to apply it to your off hand
- **Shift + left / right click** reapplies the last poison you used on that hand, if it is still in your bags
- Under the button, one line per weapon (`MH`, `OH`) shows the minutes left and, when the game reports them, the charges
- Works in combat. The list is rebuilt out of combat, so a poison picked up during a fight appears when it ends

### Poison reminders
- Out of combat, a chat line and a short banner when a weapon has no poison, a poison is about to expire, its charges are low, or your stock in the bags is running short
- Checks when you enter an instance, on a ready check and when combat ends. Each trigger has its own switch, and each threshold can be set to `0` to turn that rule off
- Stock reminders start after you apply a poison through the button, since that is how the addon learns which poison is on the weapon

### Tracker
- Three groups in the style of Blizzard's Cooldown Manager:
  - **Essentials**: your cooldowns, such as Vanish, Evasion, Sprint, Kick, Blind, Kidney Shot, Cold Blood and Death Mark
  - **Buffs**: such as Stealth, Poised, Slice and Dice, Venom and Adrenaline Rush
  - **Target**: your poison and combo debuffs on the target, such as Rupture, Garrote, Expose Armor and Deadly Poison
- Only spells your character knows are shown, so untaken talents stay out
- Cooldown sweep and countdown, stack counts, and a glow when a cooldown is ready or an aura is up
- Each group shows icons or bars. A missing aura can be hidden or dimmed, per group
- Add or remove any spell by id or name, and reorder them

### Options and commands
- `/ras` opens the options in the game's Settings window (under AddOns); every setting applies at once
- `/ras unlock` and `/ras lock` move the poison button and the tracker groups. Nothing moves in combat
- `/ras help` lists the commands; `/ras debug` lists the diagnostics (`probe`, `bags`, `spells`, `auras`, `selftest`)
- English, and Spanish on the `esES` and `esMX` clients

### Secret values
- In arenas, battlegrounds and some encounters the game hides combat data from addons. Rogue Assistant never guesses: a hidden cooldown or aura shows a `?` with a tooltip instead of a wrong number or a wrong "ready"
- The addon never reads the combat log and never acts on its own: every click does exactly one thing

### Privacy
- Everything stays on your computer. The addon sends nothing to other players or to any server. See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/Fragudev/addon-rogue-assistant/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md)
- Rogues only: on any other class the addon turns itself off

## 🧭 How to use

1. Download `RogueAssistant-<version>.zip` from the [Releases page](https://github.com/Fragudev/addon-rogue-assistant/releases) and unzip it into `World of Warcraft/<forever client folder>/Interface/AddOns/`
2. Restart the game, make sure **Rogue Assistant** is enabled in the AddOns list, and log in with a rogue
3. Type **`/ras`** to open the options, then `/ras unlock` to drag the button and the tracker groups where you want them, and `/ras lock` to lock them again
4. Left or right click a poison in the button's list to apply it to a weapon
5. If something looks wrong, run **`/ras debug probe`**, `/ras debug bags`, `/ras debug spells` and `/ras debug auras`, and include the output in your report

The full walkthrough is the [user guide](https://github.com/Fragudev/addon-rogue-assistant/blob/main/docs/USER_GUIDE.md).

## 🐞 Known limitations

- **Not tested in game.** Nothing has run on the Forever client yet, and the client APIs are assumed, not measured
- **Unconfirmed ids:** poison item ids and most spell ids are Classic Era ids. A missing icon or an unrecognised poison is the first thing to check
- **Thousand Cuts and Cutthroat are not tracked by default** (no confirmed Forever spell id). You can add them by name or id in the options
- **The secure poison button is untested in game.** Clicking a poison has to start the application and set the weapon enchant, and this has not been checked yet
- **Stock reminders need one manual application** through the button before they start
